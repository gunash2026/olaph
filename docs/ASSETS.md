## Görsel istemi

Premium isometric miniature manufacturing line for OLAPH. Three translucent frosted glass modular production stations connected by a thin polished metal conveyor from lower left to right. Neutral raw material cylinders and sheets enter; identical cognac copper cartons leave. Pale warm gray #f5f4f0, quiet editorial studio light, realistic subtle contact shadows. Copper #a76f48 only accent; white and gray materials. No text, labels, UI, logos, people, robots, purple, blue, globes or meaningless decorative spheres. Wide 3:2 composition with generous negative space.
